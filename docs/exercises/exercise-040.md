# Exercise 040 - Student Grade Status

## Statement
Ask for two grades and display their arithmetic mean and the student's status: failed for a mean below 5;
remedial assessment required for a mean of at least 5 but below 7; passed for a mean of 7 or above.

## Source code
[StudentGradeStatus.kt](../../src/exercises/conditionals/ex040/StudentGradeStatus.kt)

## How to run
Open StudentGradeStatus.kt and run its main function.

## Example

Input:
6
5

Output: <br>
Average: 5.50 <br>
Status: Remedial assessment required.

The decimal separator depends on the system locale.
